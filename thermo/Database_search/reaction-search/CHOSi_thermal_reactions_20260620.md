# CHOSi thermal reaction audit

Retrieved: 2026-06-20

Target conditions: gas phase, neutral species, 1000-2000 K. The current HD189 profile spans a wider range, but selection in this audit is based on the requested 1000-2000 K interval.

Rate convention:

`k(T) = A T^B exp(-C/T)` with molecule-cm-s units. KIDA/UMIST Kooij coefficients were converted from `alpha (T/300)^beta exp(-gamma/T)`. Molar NASA coefficients were divided by the appropriate power of Avogadro's number.

## Rejected or lower-priority alternatives

- NIST exact-channel searches returned no parsed record for reactions 1 and 3-18. Reaction 2 returned one low-temperature record, consistent with KIDA/UMIST.
- NASA TP-2129 gives `SiO + OH -> SiO2 + H` as `4.0E12 exp(-5700/RT)` cm3 mol-1 s-1. It was rejected in favor of the newer 1000-2500 K Zachariah-Tsang expression.
- KIDA values are not treated as high-temperature validation when their listed ranges stop below 500 K.
- No arbitrary high-pressure limit was invented for reactions 1 and 6; they are represented in VULCAN's low-pressure-only section.

## Warnings

Reactions 1, 4, 9-12, 14, 17, and 18 are estimates. Reactions 2, 6, 7, 13, 15, and 16 require temperature extrapolation for some or all of 1000-2000 K. These rates should be included in sensitivity tests and should not be presented as experimentally validated over the full model range.
